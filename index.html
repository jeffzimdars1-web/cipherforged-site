Forge & Fury: Uranium Front

A real-time strategy game for Android in the spirit of the classics. Build a base, mine ore, roll out armor and fight for the uranium at the heart of a 3 km island.

  • Battleground rivers, bridges, swamps, forests and towns, with special buildings to take over
  • Headquarters 15 levels, each with its own look, commander perks and power strikes
  • Factions three modern armies, each fully unique
  • Play against the computer now, with friends online later
In development for Android

The studio

Cipher Forged Studios is one developer building games in upstate New York. Every unit and building is modeled and rendered in 3D, then brought into the game by hand.
