Cipher Forged Studios Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy covers the games and apps published by Cipher Forged Studios ("we"), including Forge & Fury: Uranium Front, our real-time strategy game for Android. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

The current version of the game does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it does not use ads or analytics.

  • Your settings and game progress are saved only on your device.
  • To check for updates, the game downloads a small version file. Like any web request, this shares your device's IP address with the server that hosts the file. We don't store or use it.

Online play (coming later)

When we add online play with friends, you will be able to create a player account. For that we will store a player name, an email address for signing in, your friends list and match results with our backend provider, Supabase. This data will be used only to run the online features. We will update this page before those features go live.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Children

Our games are not made for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes everything it saved on your device. Once accounts exist, you will be able to ask us to delete yours at any time by email.
